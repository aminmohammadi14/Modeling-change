The archive is a reproducible numerical repair, not a completed proof of the full production terminal value. A nonzero whole-master warm start, incremental symbolic block insertion, external hard watchdog, exhaustive presolve tuning, scaled residual breakdown for every dual-row family, and full-scale cost-dual replay for all uncertainty realizations are not demonstrated. Generation checksums detect corruption; they are not digital signatures authenticating arbitrary third-party checkpoints. The exact arithmetic part checks the assembled LP coefficients, not uncertainty about the paper's physical inputs.

## Controlled production result

The published 25 s-per-solve three-column benchmark used all 1,882 uncertainty coordinates. It started with one active column and kept all three saved patterns. Master size fell from 639,163 to 217,591 variables and from 1,802,864 to 612,826 constraints. Total measured driver times were 252.84 s archived and 257.05 s on the validation host. Process peak RSS at the recorded MIP result was 1,509,556,224 bytes; the original peak was not measured. The root LP timed out without a solution and displayed 0.0. No rational upper proof was available. The MIP returned a feasible zero incumbent, TIME_LIMIT and +Inf global bound. No all-column witness or full coverage certificate resulted. The oracle retained LB 1,682,535.613777655 and UB +Inf. See `validation/benchmark.json` and `validation/three_column_benchmark/`. An earlier development smoke check used a 20 s cap; it likewise returned unresolved. These are not solver speedup measurements on identical hardware. The last durability-only change commits the pricing bound before the local column replay; it does not affect the measured separation model.

## Release validation

The independent 5-scenario × 3-pattern cost replay obtained 11 optimal LPs with matching primal and dual costs, and four infeasible LPs with audited rays. See `validation/COST_LP_AUDIT.csv`. The 29 normalized-ray checks include a comparison of the two normalization formulations. A positive master candidate with an inconclusive feasibility-ray check can now trigger a second fixed-pattern LP with the original economic objective for the same realization. The application checks the resulting primal or infeasibility evidence.

A new generation inherits its parent's solver trace. Reading a locally generated unrestricted cost bound requires both its receipt and a matching row of the committed trace. The included `validation/migrated_checkpoint` was generated with the distributed wrapper and recovered in a second process. Its lower bound is 1682535.613777655, its scenario hash is `e1e8408549e2cd79be3bfa953443ef896b78e49e0615f8d82a189f95278275c6`, and it retains all three archived patterns.
